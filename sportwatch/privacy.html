Privacy policy

This text applies to the SportWatch app. The publisher is In Coetus Salvo (incoetussalvo.se).

Svenska

Information

SportWatch collects no personal data. The app has no login and no account. It has no ads, and the ad SDK has been removed. The app does not use GPS or other location information, and it does not use the camera. The app works offline.

Settings are stored only locally on the device. The only permission is INTERNET.

Health and safety

SportWatch is not medical advice. The Beep Test and the Cooper Test are done at your own risk. Consult a doctor if you are unsure about your health, and stop if you feel unwell. VO2max is only an estimate. The same information is in the terms of use.

See also the shared privacy policy for all our apps.

Contact

Questions about the app use the same contact method as the rest of In Coetus Salvo: markoluttinen73@gmail.com. That address and the contact page are the ones already used on the website. More about the app is on the support page.
