Delete account

Developer: In Coetus Salvo (Marko Luttinen). This page applies to the Adventure Engine app (Interaktiva Äventyr), the only one of our apps that has accounts.

Svenska · Privacy policy · Support

Which apps have an account?

Only Adventure Engine has an account. Digital Skrivbok, SportWatch and 1001 Storys have no accounts. Data in those apps stays on the device and can be removed by uninstalling the app.

How to delete your Adventure Engine account

There are two ways.

In the app

In the app, under account settings.

By email

Email markoluttinen73@gmail.com from the email address that belongs to the account. Use Delete account as the subject.

What is deleted and what is kept

The account and saved games are deleted. Minimised purchase and order records are kept for 7 years under the Swedish Accounting Act, bokföringslagen (1999:1078) Chapter 7, Section 2. The same rule is in the privacy policy for apps.

We delete without undue delay, and at the latest within one month.
