Frequently asked questions

Digital Skrivbok (Easy Learning)

Does my child need an account?

No. The app has no sign-in. You create a profile with a name, an avatar and a colour, and it is saved on the device.

How do I delete a profile?

Press and hold the profile name on the "Who is practising?" screen and confirm. The profile and all progress are deleted permanently and cannot be restored.

Does the app show ads, or can my child buy anything?

No. The app shows no ads and has no in-app purchases.

Where is the privacy information?

In our shared privacy policy for all apps, with a dedicated section about children.

SportWatch

Does the app work offline?

Yes. SportWatch works offline.

Does the app collect anything about me?

No. The app has no sign-in, no account and no ads. Settings are stored only on the device.

How do I reset my settings?

Clear the app's data or reinstall the app.

Are the Beep Test and Cooper Test medical advice?

No. The tests are done at your own risk. Consult a doctor if you are unsure about your health, and stop if you feel unwell. Read more at SportWatch support.

1001 Storys

Where are my stories stored?

Saved stories, favourites and illustrations are on your device. If you uninstall the app they are removed.

What is sent when a story is created?

What you fill in (for example the child's first name, animal, place and theme) is sent via our server to OpenAI to create the story. You can use just a first name or an invented name.

Stories are created by AI. Can they contain mistakes?

Yes. We recommend that an adult reads the story before the child listens or reads.

How do I cancel my subscription?

The subscription is handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. Cancel it in your subscription settings in the store. We cannot cancel it for you.

How do I turn off the daily reminder?

Turn off the reminder in the app, or turn off notifications for the app in your phone's settings.

Adventure Engine (Interaktiva Äventyr)

Do I need an account?

Yes, to sign in, keep purchases and save your game to your account. Sign-in is handled by Clerk.

How do I pay, and are my card details stored?

Payment is handled by Stripe. We do not store card details.

My purchase does not show up. What should I do?

Sign in with the same account you bought with. If that does not help, email us with the account's email address and roughly when you bought.

How do I delete my account and data?

Email us and ask for deletion. Read about your rights in the privacy policy.

PerimeterGuard

PerimeterGuard has not been released yet.
