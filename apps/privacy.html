2. Common base for all apps

We collect as little personal data as possible. We do not sell personal data. Our apps currently show no advertising and use no third-party analytics or tracking tools. What each app actually processes is described in that app's section below.

Legal bases

  • Contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): account, purchases and cloud-saved progress, where you have an account with us (Adventure Engine) or a subscription (1001 Storys).
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)): answering support requests, keeping the services secure and preventing misuse.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)): bookkeeping of payments under the Swedish Accounting Act (bokföringslagen).
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): for example daily reminders in 1001 Storys. You can withdraw consent at any time. For children under 13, consent from a parent or guardian is required, see section 7.

3. Children and young people

Digital Skrivbok is an app for children. We want children to be able to use our children's apps without personal data leaving the device.

  • Age limit (Art. 8 GDPR): When an online service is offered directly to a child and processing relies on consent, the consent of the child's parent or guardian is required for children under 16. Sweden has lowered the limit to 13 years (Data Protection Act, lag 2018:218). Children under 13 therefore cannot give valid consent themselves, and we ask for consent from a parent or guardian.
  • Parents and guardians: Children's apps are used under the responsibility of a parent or guardian. We encourage guardians to manage profiles, purchases and web links themselves.
  • No advertising, profiling or tracking aimed at children: Our children's apps show no advertising, build no ad profile and do not track children across apps or websites. We do not sell children's data.
  • Apple App Store, Kids category: We design Digital Skrivbok to follow Apple's rules for the Kids category: no third-party advertising and no third-party analytics, and no transmission of personal data that could identify the child. The app may contain links out to the web. [ATT FYLLA I / TO CONFIRM] Verify against Apple's current guidelines before submission.
  • Google Play Families Policy: We design Digital Skrivbok to follow Google's Families Policy: correctly declared target audience, no advertising, no sensitive permissions (the app declares no Android permissions) and no collection of personal data that could identify children. [ATT FYLLA I / TO CONFIRM] Complete the target audience and Data safety in Play Console and verify against the current policy.
  • Other apps: 1001 Storys is aimed at parents and adults, not directly at children. The child's name is entered by an adult, stays on the device and is never sent to the server or the AI. Adventure Engine requires an account. The minimum age for an account (16 or 18) is not yet set; see the Adventure Engine section. SportWatch collects no personal data.

4. How each app works

Digital Skrivbok (Easy Learning)

Educational app where children practise letters, sounds, handwriting and reading. Package name: se.incoetussalvo.easylearning.skrivbok.

  • Stored on the device: profile name (up to 20 characters, often the child's first name), chosen avatar and colour, and progress, rewards and word progress per profile. Everything is saved locally with AsyncStorage.
  • Sent to us or third parties: no personal data about the child. Profile name, avatar, colour and progress stay only on the device. We found no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or sign-in.
  • Updates: the app checks for and downloads updates from Expo (expo-updates, u.expo.dev). This involves technical connection data such as IP address and app version, but no personal data about the child.
  • Permissions: the app declares no Android permissions and does not use camera, microphone or location. Read-aloud uses the device's own text-to-speech (expo-speech).
  • Links: the app may contain links out to the web.
  • Delete: press and hold a profile name on the "Who is practising?" screen to remove the profile and its progress. Uninstalling the app removes everything.
  • Legal basis: because the data exists only on the device, we are normally not the controller of it. For technical update calls, legitimate interest applies.

SportWatch

Interval and training timer. Package name: com.incoetussalvo.sportswatch. The published app has no camera, no location, no microphone, no ads and no data collection. INTERNET is its only permission.

  • Personal data: none is collected. The app has no sign-in, no account and no server. Settings and profiles are stored only locally on the device.
  • Ads: the app has no ads.
  • Permissions: INTERNET is the only permission. The app does not use the camera, microphone or location. The app works offline.
  • More information: see the app's own pages: privacy policy and support.

1001 Storys (1001 Story Nights)

App where parents create personalised bedtime stories with AI. Package name: com.storynights.app.

  • Stays on the device: the child's name and the second hero's name, saved stories, favourites, settings and reminders. The name is never sent to the server or the AI.
  • Sent when a story is created: the choices (animal, character, place, theme, age level, language, length, era and tone) and, for a continuation, the previous chapter text without names. These go to our server on Replit and on to OpenAI (text and images). For images, the title and opening are sent, without names.
  • Free-text fields: do not enter personal data in free-text fields.
  • Our server: the server stores no stories or choices. Technical logs contain time, path and status. The IP address is used temporarily to protect against abuse.
  • Purchases: purchases are handled by Apple, Google and RevenueCat with an anonymous ID. We do not receive your card details.
  • Read-aloud: no OpenAI voice is used. Read-aloud uses the device's built-in voice.

Adventure Engine (Interaktiva Äventyr)

Interactive text adventures as a web app and mobile app, with free adventures and adventures bought once.

  • Account: sign-in and account are handled by Clerk. Sign-in is by email and password, or by a one-time code sent by email, with Clerk's bot protection (captcha). There is no Google or Apple sign-in. The app shows your email address in the account view. [ATT FYLLA I / TO CONFIRM] State the minimum age for an account (16 or 18).
  • Purchases: payment is handled by Stripe. Our database stores your account ID, which adventure you bought, Stripe's session and payment IDs, amount, payment status, time, which version of the terms you accepted, and that you confirmed immediate delivery and the loss of the right of withdrawal.
  • Saved games: when you are signed in, your game position (current scene, visited scenes, choices and counters, timestamps) is saved to your account so you can continue on another device. Progress and statistics are also stored locally on the device.
  • PDF bundles: when you buy a PDF bundle, we store the country, a short-lived download token (as a hash), account ID and expiry. The PDF files are stored in Google Cloud Storage. Download links are not logged in clear text.
  • Logs: the server logs only method, URL (with the download token removed) and status code.
  • Ads and analytics: no advertising and no analytics SDK were found in the code.
